# BLUE SCHOLARS [https://scholars.blue]

A New **Blue Scholars** website. Group active 2002-2016. On musical hiatus since 2013 and performing hiatus since 2016. Lost original domain URL (bluescholars.com, 2002-2021) due to lack of funds and oversight. Currently a legacy group with IP and musical catalog (available for streaming, licensing, and physical copy manufacturing), backstock of old and new merchandise inventory. 

## Primary Objectives:
* Move current site hosted at <https://bluescholars.squarespace.com> to self-hosted site at <https://bluescholars.netlify.com>.
* Current site status: [![Netlify Status](https://api.netlify.com/api/v1/badges/227a52a1-11fd-490b-bfab-55a4c1dac1dc/deploy-status)](https://app.netlify.com/projects/bluescholars/deploys)
* Stabilizing scholars.blue as Blue Scholars' canonical home.
* Portal for music catalog on streaming/download platforms.
* Move merch again. Migrate shop and inventory to new platform. Exploring options: [Shopify](https://shopify.com), [Snipcart](https://snipcart.com), self-coded or open-source projects.

## Secondary Objectives:
* Archived previous versions of bluescholars.com
* Provide access to official media content/social media pages
* Direct audience to group members' other music work ( [Prometheus Brown](https://prometheusbrown.bandcamp.com) and [Sabzi](https://sabzi.bandcamp.com)), digital footprint, and other projects.
* Indexing and rebuilding SEO rankings following loss of bluescholars.com URL.
